RESULTS:
3. Results 3.1. Study Selection A literature search using PubMed, Scopus, WOS, and Medline revealed 5710 articles, 2511 of which were duplicates. Title and abstract screening were performed on 3199 articles, and 364 articles were retrieved. Then, we reviewed them at the full-text screening stage. Finally, 10 studies were included in this systematic review and meta-analysis. The PRISMA flowchart is shown in Figure 1 . Figure 1 PRISMA flowchart of the study. 3.2. Study Characteristics This systematic review included three studies [ 35 , 36 , 37 ]. Two of the included studies were conference abstracts of randomized controlled trials that were registered under protocol IDs NCT03624309 and NCT03726736 , and one was a completed randomized trial. They were conducted in China. The sample ranged from 40 to 57 in the anlotinib plus docetaxel group and from 31 to 58 in the docetaxel group. The total number of randomized patients was 151 for the docetaxel + anlotinib group and 132 for the docetaxel-only group. The median age ranged from 54 to 62.1 in the anlotinib-plus-docetaxel group and from 58 to 62.9 in the docetaxel-only group. All of the included patients had received platinum-based chemotherapy as a first-line therapy. The summary and baseline characteristics of the included studies are shown in Table 1 and Table 2 . Efficacy Outcomes 3.3.1. Progression-Free Survival (PFS) The data of the three studies, including 151 participants in the intervention group and 132 in the control group, were analyzed and showed a significant difference in the PFS between patients who received anlotinib plus docetaxel and patients who received only docetaxel (MD = 2.98, 95% CI = 1.95–4.00; p < 0.00001). The pooled studies were homogenous ( p = 0.81, I 2 = 0%) ( Figure 2 ). Figure 2 Meta-analysis of progression-free survival [ 35 , 36 , 37 ]. 3.3.2. Objective Response Rate (ORR) The data of the three studies, including 151 patients in the intervention group and 132 patients in the control group, were analyzed and showed a significant difference in the ORR between patients who received anlotinib and docetaxel and patients who received only docetaxel (RR = 3.04, 95% CI = 1.77–5.24; p < 0.00001). The pooled studies were homogenous ( p = 0.81, I 2 = 0%) ( Figure 3 ). Figure 3 Meta-analysis of objective response rate [ 35 , 36 , 37 ]. 3.3.3. Disease Control Rate (DCR) The data of the three studies, including 151 participants in the intervention group and 132 patients in the control group, were analyzed and showed a significant difference in the DCR between patients who received anlotinib and docetaxel and patients who received only docetaxel (RR = 1.58, 95% CI = 1.34–1.87; p < 0.00001). The pooled studies were homogenous ( p = 0.90, I 2 = 0%) ( Figure 4 ). Figure 4 Meta-analysis of disease control rate [ 35 , 36 , 37 ].
