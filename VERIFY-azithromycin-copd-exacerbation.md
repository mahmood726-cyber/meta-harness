# VERIFY - azithromycin-copd-exacerbation

Primary outcome: acute COPD exacerbations during study follow-up.

Poolable trial quotes:

- Albert et al., PMID 21864166:
  > "The frequency of exacerbations was 1.48 exacerbations per patient-year in the azithromycin group, as compared with 1.83 per patient-year in the placebo group (P=0.01), and the hazard ratio for having an acute exacerbation of COPD per patient-year in the azithromycin group was 0.73 (95% CI, 0.63 to 0.84; P<0.001)."

- COLUMBUS, PMID 24746000:
  > "We recorded 84 exacerbations in patients in the azithromycin group compared with 129 in those in the placebo group."
  > "After adjustment, azithromycin resulted in a significant reduction in the exacerbation rate versus placebo (0.58, 95% CI 0.42-0.79; p=0.001)."
