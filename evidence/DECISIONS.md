## Served scale != declared estimand (2026-09-25; decided by the lane under delegated authority, 2026-09-25)
Prompted by the adversarial second review, which found the rule applied unevenly: M-02 was rejected, while M-03 and M-04 were confirmed.
- **The number is the defect** when a row served on another scale (OR or HR) enters a pool whose other rows are on the declared scale, and the counts that give the declared scale are printed. M-02 is this case: the OR makes the k=4 hyperglycaemia pool `INCOMPATIBLE (ODDS_RATIO + RISK_RATIO)`. The ruling is CANDIDATE_REJECTED with a count-derived row, queued for signature.
- **The label is the defect** when the row is the whole outcome (k=1), carries its true scale on the row, and the pooled output reports that scale. M-03 and M-04 (FREEDOM, printed HRs) are this case. The number is confirmed. An open question offers the owner a choice: relabel the declared estimand, or serve the RR derived from printed counts. Either option changes served text, so neither lands unsigned.
