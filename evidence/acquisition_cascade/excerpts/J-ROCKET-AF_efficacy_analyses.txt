pp_on_treatment	In the primary efficacy analysis in the per-protocol population, while on treatment, stroke or non-CNS systemic embolism oc- curred at a rate of 1.26% per year in patients receiving rivar- oxaban, compared with 2.61% per year in warfarin-receiving patients (HR 0.49; 95% CI 0.24–1.00; P=0.050; Figure 3A, Table 3).
itt_including_30_day_follow_up	In the ITT population analysis including 30-day follow-up, the primary efficacy endpoint occurred at a rate of 2.38% per year and 2.91% per year in patients receiving rivaroxaban and warfarin, respectively (HR 0.82; 95% CI 0.46–1.45; Figure S2).
itt_on_treatment	In the on-treatment analysis of the ITT population, the primary efficacy endpoint occurred at a rate of 1.26% per year and 2.60% per year in patients receiving rivaroxaban and warfarin, respec- tively (HR 0.48; 95% CI 0.23–1.00).
