# VERIFY - corticosteroids-cap-mortality

Primary outcome: all-cause mortality at 28 to 60 days / in hospital.

Poolable trial quotes:

- CAPE COD hydrocortisone trial, PMID 36942789:
  > "By day 28, death had occurred in 25 of 400 patients (6.2%; 95% confidence interval [CI], 3.9 to 8.6) in the hydrocortisone group and in 47 of 395 patients (11.9%; 95% CI, 8.7 to 15.1) in the placebo group (absolute difference, -5.6 percentage points; 95% CI, -9.6 to -1.7; P = 0.006)."

- Torres et al. methylprednisolone trial, PMID 25688779:
  > "In-hospital mortality did not differ between the 2 groups (6 patients [10%] in the methylprednisolone group vs 9 patients [15%] in the placebo group; P = .37); the difference between groups was 5% (95% CI, -6% to 17%)."

- Meduri et al. low-dose methylprednisolone trial, PMID 35723686 / NCT01283009 posted results:
  > "60-day Mortality; Died on or prior to study day 60; Arm 1: Inactive Substance 50/287; Arm 2: Methylprednisolone 47/297; Odds Ratio (OR) 0.90; 95% CI 0.58 to 1.40."

Notes:

- Quote sources: PubMed abstracts for PMIDs 36942789 and 25688779; ClinicalTrials.gov v2 posted results in `cache/corticosteroids-cap-mortality/records.json` for NCT01283009, linked in PubMed record PMID 35723686.
- No computation performed here.
