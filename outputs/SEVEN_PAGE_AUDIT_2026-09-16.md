**The stale "OR and RR are one compatibility class" sentence — found, two pages, same topic.** On `reviews/corticosteroids-cap-mortality/` (Harms tab, outcome *Hyperglycaemia*) and on its served, unlinked blinded mirror `m/m0594e053/` (HTTP 200): "Effect-measure class FIRST_EVENT_RATIO · Effect-measure labels pooled OR, RR … ⚠ Pooled trials report different effect-measure labels (OR + RR), combined as first-event relative ratios. This is an approximation, not an identity: a hazard ratio and a cumulative risk ratio are not the same number…". The pool is Torres 11/61 vs 7/59 (count-RR) with Blum 2015 **OR** 1.96 [1.31, 2.93]; hyperglycaemia is a common event (≈11–19%), so OR≠RR materially. **What it contradicts:** (a) the site's own index page and `external_agreement.json._doc`: "an odds ratio sits further from 1 than a risk ratio for common events … comparing them … is comparing different quantities"; (b) the iv-iron page, which suppresses a pool across estimand classes; (c) the sentence's own explanation, which is the HR-vs-RR template fired for an OR — the text argues about hazard ratios while the labels say OR. The primary (mortality) outcome on the same page is a clean RR/RR pool; only the harms outcome carries the defect. 1 of 32 topics, 2 of 64+32 served pages (MEASURED by regex over all served review pages; the `m/` mirrors were checked via the repo copy, which byte-matches the served manifest).

**Should a k=2 pooled row with CI 0.03–33.89 be served?** No — not as a pooled row. At k=2 the HKSJ multiplier is t₁ = 12.71 (vs 1.96), so the interval is a statement about one degree of freedom, not about the drugs. Evidence: on corticosteroids-cap the two trials are concordant (0.53 and 0.65, τ²=0, Q≈0.1) and the pool is still 0.04–8.26; on spironolactone, RALES+EMPHASIS alone — two large, concordant, individually significant trials — pool to 0.72 [0.32, 1.61] under the same method (MEASURED). **Every k=2 HKSJ pool on this site will cross the null regardless of the data.** The page already applies this logic to the prediction interval ("not estimated (k=2): it requires a t-quantile on a single degree of freedom and is not reliable") and then serves the CI computed on the same quantile. The honest render, by the site's own standard: at k=2, show both trials, refuse the pooled CI with reason code `K2_SINGLE_DF` (or serve it only under the same "not reliable at k=2" label the PI gets), show the common-effect sensitivity as sensitivity, and never emit "flipped to non-significant" language — the flip is the estimator's, not the evidence's. Ticagrelor is worse than that general case: the two trials point in opposite directions (0.84 vs 1.47, I²≈78% by my DL-Q), so even the direction is undetermined; that row should be a refusal with reason `DIRECTION_CONFLICT_K2` and PLATO shown as the honest k=1 with PHILO as the named remainder. Mahmood's rule applies exactly: an honest k=1 plus a named remainder beats a k=2 that says nothing.
