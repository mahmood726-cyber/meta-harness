# k-gap phase 2: PMDA, Health Canada and EMA Policy 0070 (13 Oct 2026). No route can be run by the lane

## PMDA (Japan)

**Terms**, read 13 Oct 2026 at https://www.pmda.go.jp/english/0013.html:
- **Licence.** Content is under the **Public Data License 1.0 (PDL 1.0)**, with source citation required and edits marked as edits. PDL 1.0 is the Japanese government open-data licence and is designed to be compatible with CC BY 4.0.
- **Retrieval.** "Automated crawling or download applications are also not permitted." Bulk or frequent downloads are prohibited, and downloads must be "the minimum necessary".

**Status: not attempted: terms forbid automated download; skipped by Mahmood 13 Oct.** No PMDA document was downloaded or held. The three English review reports identified by hand from PMDA's listing (Praluent 2016 `files/000224582.pdf`, Repatha 2016 `files/000217925.pdf`, Actemra 2022 `files/000246328.pdf`) are recorded here as pointers only. The PDL 1.0 licence question is moot.
