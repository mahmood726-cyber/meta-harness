# Notice for Mahmood: the registered dpp4-mace-t2d comparator never pools 3-point MACE

Lane G1, 2026-10-03. **A notice only.** Nothing served or registered was changed, and nothing is signed.

**What.** dpp4-mace-t2d's primary outcome is 3-point MACE. Its registered comparator is PMID 34754403 (World J Cardiol
2021, "Cardiovascular efficacy and safety of dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitors: A meta-analysis of cardiovascular outcome
trials"). It pools "data from 6 trials in a total of 52520 patients", but only for components and other outcomes:
- fatal and non-fatal MI, RR 1.02 (0.94-1.11);
- stroke, RR 0.96 (0.85-1.08);
- heart-failure hospitalisation, RR 1.09 (0.92-1.29);
- CV death, RR 1.02 (0.93-1.11);
- revascularisation and unstable angina;
- arrhythmias.

Neither its abstract nor its held full text (verified as the named article) reports a composite or MACE result.

**Consequence.** No result on our side can agree with it, so G1 for this topic cannot be reached against this comparator.
The tracker now reports `COMPARATOR_NO_PRIMARY_RESULT` and keeps the evidence (what the comparator does pool) on the row.
It also records that the comparator states 6 trials while our list has 5.

**Your decision:** register a different open-access comparator that pools 3-point MACE across the DPP-4 CVOTs, or accept
this topic as G1-inapplicable against PMID 34754403.
