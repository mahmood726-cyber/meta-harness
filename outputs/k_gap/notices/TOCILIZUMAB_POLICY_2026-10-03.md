# Notice for Mahmood: two policy questions on the remaining tocilizumab trials

Lane g1/tocilizumab-finish, 2026-10-03. **A notice only.** Nothing is signed, and nothing served changed.

The count is 6 of 19, at the limit of the sources under the current rules. Two rules decide trials whose numbers exist:

1. **Timepoint window.** ImmCoVA's own open report states "6 deaths up to day 29, two in each arm"; REACT's row is 2/22
   vs 2/27. Under the lane rule ("another day is never matched as day 28") it stays open. If day 29 is accepted as the
   day-28 window, it is one trial; its arm sizes would still need a stated source.
2. **Kaplan-Meier percentages.** CORIMUNO-TOCI-ICU, and COV-AID's IL-6 vs no-IL-6 contrast, report day-28 mortality only
   as KM percentages, and AACT only as percentages. The rule is that a converted percentage is never a count. Relaxing
   it would admit derived numbers, which is not recommended.

Neither is changed here.
