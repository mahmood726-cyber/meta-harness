## Central framing (correct, and already half-built)
Traditional meta-analysis publishes a conclusion with supporting references; this publishes a conclusion with a **machine-checkable proof of how the evidence became that conclusion**. Already in place: source spans on pooled values, refusal states with counterfactual estimates (the iv-iron refusal rendering is the document's own example of what a refusal should look like), byte-identical replay from a protocol SHA, the STALE banner, declared strands, the claim graph.

## Adopted now (today's findings demand them regardless of the strategy)
1. **The trial-family node as the fundamental object.** The most-repeated finding across 19 search audits and 2 extraction audits: publications counted as trials; extensions counted as new randomisations (FREEDOM crossover extension; DIRECT open-label extension); a COLCOT cost-effectiveness paper counted as an RCT; a LoDoCo2 secondary counted again; STEP 6's parent missing while its post-hoc was counted. **Every "n trials met eligibility" figure in the corpus is wrong because the unit is wrong.** The node links registry entries, protocol, SAP, primary paper, supplement, secondary publications, conference abstracts, corrections, FDA/EMA material and follow-up reports; eligibility is decided once per family; reports carry `publication_role`. Owner: lane SF (families before eligibility) + the G1 page lane on glp1 first.
2. **The source ladder replaces terminal `OUTCOME_NOT_IN_SOURCE`.** Abstract → full article → supplement → registry results → protocol/SAP → regulatory (FDA/EMA) → secondary publications → author query; the only terminal state is `RESULT NOT LOCATED after sources A–G checked`, listing the attempts. One mechanism with Mahmood's full-text reachability ladder (PMC/Europe PMC, supplements, publisher OA, author manuscripts, FDA/EMA, CT.gov/AACT) and the source hierarchy (`source_level` 1–5; older metas are pointers only). Owner: EX2/TE under the reproducible-model contract; glp1 first (ELIXA, FREEDOM-CVO).
3. **The outcome-missingness map.** Per eligible trial family: `MACE prospectively specified / measured / reported / extractable / in primary pool` (five columns, each with a span or a typed absence). Cheap and novel: publication-bias reasoning becomes "of all independently discovered eligible families, which prespecified this outcome and where are their results?" instead of "are small studies missing?" — and it supplies the honest denominator the GRADE publication-bias domain currently lacks. **Built for glp1 as part of layer 5.**

## Build as the signature: the disagreement decomposer
Today's proof of need: we reported 0.86; Hasebe 2025 and the 2026 NMA report 0.86; it was coincidence — we omit ELIXA, FLOW and FREEDOM-CVO and include SOUL; different missing and added trials cancelled in the centre. Nothing on the page could show that. The decomposer attributes any difference (or agreement) to **membership, endpoint definition, effect estimate, follow-up choice and statistical model**, then progressively reproduces the comparator: their trials + their method → their result; our search + their method; our trials + our harmonisation; our model. Agreement becomes evidence instead of reassurance. **Design note starts against the glp1 / Hasebe pair** (fully characterised: route restriction, ten-trial set, HR 0.86 [0.82, 0.91], I² 31.3%). Owner: CP4 after landing 3; design note first.

## Design constraint recorded now
The `PROPOSED → VERIFIED → ADJUDICATED` state machine for AI-derived objects is right, and **human adjudication is the throughput ceiling**. Design for it: what fraction of objects require it; the queue at 32 pages; which classes are adjudicated by a deterministic post-check instead. Under the reproducible-model contract, a span-verified model judgement with a citable basis may not need a human; an unverifiable one always does. That distinction decides whether this scales. Measurement to add to the glp1 proof: `n objects requiring human adjudication of N model-derived objects` on the page.
