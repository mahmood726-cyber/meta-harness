   - Syntactic Shape: An `ast.Call` to `datetime.now()` or `datetime.utcnow()` that writes its output directly into a dictionary/formatter building a reproducible artifact (like a manifest, candidate snapshot, or payload), bypassing a pinned timestamp variable.
   - False-Positive Risk: Low if specifically targeting output serialization blocks of artifacts explicitly claiming determinism. High in general utility code, as generic logging/profiling naturally requires live clock times.
