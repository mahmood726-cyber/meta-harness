# LANE EN — name the estimand, do not imply a uniform biological event: trial-defined composites, prior-disease-stage and background-therapy compatibility dimensions, subgroup-as-trial disclosure

Report file: `LANE-EN-REPORT.md`.

## The failures (external audit of all 22 pooled pages, 2026-09-16)

1. `sglt2-ckd-progression`: outcome is named "CKD progression / kidney composite outcome" as if one event. The three pooled primaries differ in components (DAPA-CKD ≥50% eGFR decline / ESKD / renal or CV death; CREDENCE doubling of creatinine / ESKD / renal or CV death; EMPA-KIDNEY kidney-disease progression or CV death). Note also that the page's `estimand_exclusions` refuse DECLARE-TIMI 58 for "composite includes cardiovascular death" while all three pooled composites include CV death — the exclusion reason contradicts the pool; do NOT change the pool, but the outcome must read as "trial-defined major kidney / cardiorenal composite (components differ; listed per trial)" and the exclusion reason must be made true or marked as contradicted (report which).
2. `colchicine-recurrent-pericarditis`: CORP (first recurrence) and CORP-2 (multiple recurrences) pooled; prior-disease stage (post-first-episode vs established recurrent) must be an explicit compatibility-key dimension, disclosed per trial.
3. `metformin-pcos-ovulation`: at least two pooled inputs are metformin ADDED TO clomifene vs clomifene+placebo (e.g. Moll 2006, PMID 16769748), not metformin monotherapy vs placebo; background ovulation-induction therapy must be a compatibility/subgroup dimension, disclosed per trial and in the question/estimand text.
4. `statins-primary-prevention-elderly`: one of the two pooled rows (PMID 20404379) is the JUPITER ≥70-years SUBGROUP, not a trial designed around the question; the page must say so on the row and in the outcome estimand (`population` field), and the result must not read as "two trials".

Where it lives: `topics/<slug>.json` (outcome `name`, `estimand`, `population`, `timepoint`; `compat_key` inputs), `harness/design_key.py` / the compatibility key builder (grep `compat_key` in `harness/pipeline.py`), `harness/page.py` compatibility-key rendering ("Compatibility key (pooling contract)"), `harness/manuscript.py`. Read `harness/estmeasure.py` docstring and `tests/test_compat_key.py` first.

2. Per-trial component listing for composite outcomes: a `components` list on each pooled row for `sglt2-ckd-progression` (from the cached abstracts) and the outcome name changed to "Trial-defined major kidney / cardiorenal composite (components differ by trial)". Subgroup-as-trial: a typed row field `evidence_unit` ∈ {`trial`, `prespecified_subgroup`, `post_hoc_subgroup`} rendered on the row; `k` prose becomes "k = 2 (1 trial + 1 pre-specified subgroup of JUPITER)" wherever k is stated for statins. 4. Rebuild affected pages; `reproduce_review.py` each; quote before/after outcome names, key rows and the sglt2-ckd exclusion reason.
