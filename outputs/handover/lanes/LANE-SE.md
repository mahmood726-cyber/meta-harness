# LANE SE — eligibility scope and search scope must be THE SAME OBJECT: a page cannot claim open eligibility, seed retrieval from known PMIDs, and conclude "exactly k are eligible". Gate + corpus sweep; the search itself is Phase 2B.

Report file: `LANE-SE-REPORT.md`.

## The failure (topics 5, 6, 11; CLAIMED until re-measured)
- `doac-vte-recurrence`: protocol = P/I/C/design only (adult acute symptomatic DVT/PE, a DOAC, VKA comparator, randomised; no phase restriction; outcome reporting not a criterion). Retrieval = six named phase III PMIDs. Page: "6 met eligibility; all 6 were pooled." Three trials satisfy the written criteria and are ABSENT from the ledger (not excluded — absent): Botticelli-DVT (18541000; 520 pts; apixaban doses vs LMWH/VKA; 9/358 vs 3/118 evaluable), ODIXa-DVT (17576867), EINSTEIN-DVT dose-ranging (18621928; 543 randomised; per-protocol events). Exploratory 9-study pool ≈0.896 [0.754, 1.065] — a protocol/search integrity defect, not a reversal. Do NOT pool them (dose-ranging, evaluable/per-protocol sets, imaging endpoints) — the pipeline must be: screen in on P/I/C/design → recover → compatibility → pool or typed refusal; the system skips step one.
- `noac-vs-warfarin-af-stroke`: J-ROCKET AF (22664783 / NCT00494871; 1,280 Japanese NVAF; rivaroxaban vs warfarin; primary = stroke/systemic embolism; HR 0.49 [0.24, 1.00], per-protocol on-treatment) absent from the screening universe; also ARISTOTLE-J (222 pts) and PETRO. Retrieval enumerated four known PMIDs; the registry query was "atrial fibrillation + edoxaban warfarin". Sensitivity if added: k=5 ≈0.791 [0.650, 0.962], τ²≈0.0122 — robust. Also the 12-Sept post-hoc dose amendment (dabigatran 150 not 110; edoxaban 60 not 30, to match the comparator) changed the estimand after the data were known: the title/PICO say "DOACs vs warfarin", the analysis is standard-dose; fix = rename the question OR prespecify standard-dose and lower-dose strata; either way state the rule, disclose both answers were known, report both.
- `metformin-pcos-ovulation`: Nestler 1998 (9637806; 61 obese PCOS; 12/35 vs 1/26 monotherapy; then 19/21 vs 2/25 after CC) absent entirely.
- Corpus: 28 of 32 pages are KNOWN_ITEM or TITLE_SEEDED (MEASURED by integrator) while their protocols state open P/I/C/design eligibility.
