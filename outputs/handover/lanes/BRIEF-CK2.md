## The central finding (external audit of `colchicine-postop-af`, hash `1d645bc2ec5d8f3c`; every item CLAIMED until you re-measure it from the committed objects; the integrator has MEASURED the arithmetic lines)

Three eligibility layers on one page, and they disagree:
- **Prose protocol** (`protocols/colchicine-postop-af.md`): "double-blind OR placebo-controlled RCTs".
- **Executable screening rule** (`topics/colchicine-postop-af.json` → `harness/screen.py::screen_record`): RCT + colchicine + eligible comparator — no blinding or placebo condition fires (check whether `X-DESIGN` is configured for this topic; the audit says it is not).
- **Rendered compatibility key**: broader still.
- And the page states: "The prose protocol and executable config agree on the checked dimensions (estimand, analysis set, design masking)." For design masking that is FALSE: **END-AF (PMID 27502857) is prospective, randomised, OPEN-LABEL, colchicine vs no colchicine — and it is pooled.**

Consequences on the same page:
1. **Zarpelon (PMID 27223641) carries two incompatible exclusion rationales, neither correct**: screening excludes it as wrong population (paper says "myocardial revascularization"; the cardiac-surgery vocabulary misses it), the parity section calls it multi-arm/dose-timing ambiguity. It is one randomised colchicine group vs one control, 5/71 vs 9/69, POAF 7.04% vs 13.04%. The only defensible exclusion is the protocol's blinding rule — which the page does not apply, since END-AF (same design) is pooled. Two rationales, one decision, an identically designed trial included → `ELIGIBILITY_STATE_INCONSISTENT`. Also: "myocardial revascularization" must map to cardiac surgery in the population vocabulary (`harness/lexicon.py` / topic `population_any`). Do NOT pool Zarpelon: the correct outcome of this lane is that the chain becomes executable and the page states, per trial, which layer admits/refuses it and why.
2. **Timepoint drift in two steps, key factually wrong**: protocol `in-hospital / index-admission`; Results broadens to `postoperative, in-hospital to 30 days`; COPPS-2's 61/180 vs 75/180 is formally a 3-month POAF endpoint (1- and 3-month visits). `follow_up_window = in-hospital to 30 days` does not describe every pooled row.
3. **Endpoint definition varies two orders of magnitude in duration threshold**: END-AF >5 min, END-AF Low Dose ≥5 min, COPPS-2 ≥30 s, Sarzaeem ≥10 min; surveillance windows differ. Pooling may be reasonable, but `duration_threshold` and `surveillance_window` must be compatibility / effect-modifier fields, not `single endpoint`.
4. **ITT asserted over available-case rows, third corpus instance**: the 2026 CABG trial (PMID 42132185) randomised 86+86=172, reports on 81+82=163; COCS 267 randomised, 240 analysed. Record `AVAILABLE_CASE` / `MODIFIED_ITT` per row; never promote to ITT.
5. **Harms k=1, fourth page**: served GI adverse effects = END-AF alone 44/179 vs 10/181, RR 4.45. Also reported by pooled/adjacent trials: COCS diarrhoea 25.7% vs 11.8% and abdominal pain 7% vs 1.6%; END-AF Low Dose diarrhoea 2/81 vs 2/71 (one discontinuation per arm); COPPS-2 adverse events 36/180 vs 21/180 driven by GI intolerance; the 2026 trial GI events ≈25.9% vs 8.5%. Four different constructs — diarrhoea, GI intolerance, any GI AE, discontinuation — must be separate compatible harm strands (lane CK's harms axis), never one collapsed "GI adverse effects".
6. **Heterogeneity source for the effect-modifier fields**: the 2026 trial RR 0.37 [0.21, 0.66] generates nearly all τ²; without it k=3 → 0.7943 [0.4909, 1.2851], τ²=0 (integrator MEASURED). Do not remove it; expose the modifiers: CABG-only, on-pump, preoperative loading, weight-adjusted 14-day regimen, 46.3% control-arm POAF rate, analysed-not-randomised population.
7. **Shipped proposition contradictions the gate missed** (route the mechanism to the claim graph, plant them here): (a) a limitation says cardiac-surgery trials the search never found are still missing, "such as the original COPPS trial", while the screening table contains PMID 22090167 as an included eligible record and Results lists it among screened-in-unpooled — "never found by the search" is false prose; (b) the parity text says the k gap is "not a search/extraction failure" on a page marking COCS `not extracted — the outcome's number IS in the source`.

## What to build

1. **`harness/eligibility_chain.py`** (new): one executable object per topic, `EligibilityContract`, compiled from `topics/<slug>.json` AND checked against the prose protocol's stated criteria (`harness/protocol_compiler.py` already reads protocol metadata — extend it to extract the design/masking/comparator/timepoint/analysis-set criteria as typed fields, or refuse with `PROTOCOL_CRITERION_NOT_EXECUTABLE` naming the sentence). Every dimension the protocol states must exist in the config as an executable rule; a protocol criterion with no config rule → `PROTOCOL_CONFIG_DIVERGENCE` (this fires on colchicine-postop-af design masking). The page sentence "prose protocol and executable config agree on …" is generated FROM this check and can only name dimensions that actually agreed.
2. **Per-trial admission record.** For every pooled row: `admission = {dimension → (trial_value, span, contract_value, verdict)}` for design/masking, comparator, population, timepoint/follow-up window, analysis set, endpoint definition (duration threshold, surveillance window where the topic defines them). A trial whose own values fail a contract dimension cannot sit in the pool: the build refuses with `TRIAL_FAILS_CONTRACT` (END-AF fails masking under the stated protocol). Do NOT silently drop END-AF: the honest render is the refusal block naming the dimension and the trial, and the page is UNRENDERABLE until the integrator decides (amend the protocol prospectively-labelled-retrospective, or exclude the trial) — that decision is not yours; report it.
3. **One exclusion, one rationale.** Every excluded/declared-absent trial carries exactly one typed rationale from the chain; two rationales for one trial → `ELIGIBILITY_STATE_INCONSISTENT` (Zarpelon). Fix the vocabulary mapping so "myocardial revascularization" screens as cardiac surgery; report what Zarpelon's single rationale becomes under the executable chain (expected: design/masking under the protocol rule, the same rule END-AF fails).
4. **Compatibility key derived from admission records**, never asserted: `follow_up_window`, `analysis_set`, `endpoint_definition` become the union/enumeration of per-trial values with `matched: false` where they differ (CK's mechanism) — the timepoint drift and the ITT promotion cannot survive this.
5. **Corpus sweep** `scripts/eligibility_chain_sweep.py` → `docs/eligibility_chain_sweep.json`: for all 32 topics, `PROTOCOL_CONFIG_DIVERGENCE` count by dimension, `TRIAL_FAILS_CONTRACT` count (pooled trials failing the stated protocol), `ELIGIBILITY_STATE_INCONSISTENT` count; `n of N` each, N = pooled rows / excluded rows over 32 topics.
6. Keep reach and extraction apart in every table: Sarzaeem 2014 (no PMID, never in corpus) = REACH_MISS (Phase 2B); COCS and COPPS = EXTRACTION_DEBT (in cache). Do not adopt any larger pool (the exploratory k=8 ≈0.632 [0.501, 0.798] is not a target; the contract must be fixed before any eligible set is regenerated, and that regeneration is a protocol-first Phase 2 commit).
