- `noac-vs-warfarin-af-stroke`: page says overlap "not exactly verifiable (comparator trial table not machine-exposed)"; Carnicelli 2022 / COMBINE-AF states in PROSE that it comprises all patients randomised in RE-LY, ROCKET-AF, ARISTOTLE and ENGAGE AF-TIMI 48 (71,683). - `esketamine-trd-madrs`: comparator Table 2 enumerates the same four acute-induction trials → `same phase III acute-induction evidence set, different extraction and statistical implementation`.
- `doac-vte-recurrence`: van Es 2014 used Hokusai's on-treatment-period data; harness uses overall-study HR 0.89 → even on identical trials the quantities differ: "closely matching synthesis of the same phase III programmes", never exact arithmetic verification. And van Es excluded phase II dose-finding trials — scope-matched only if our protocol is restricted to phase III; the page tries both.
- **Recency**: `colchicine-secondary-cv-prevention` benchmarks against a 2022 meta (RR 0.65 [0.38, 0.77], 15 RCTs, I²≈70%) published before CLEAR SYNERGY; a 2026 review (search to June 2026; 8 trials; RR 0.75 [0.62, 0.89]) is broader (cerebrovascular, usual-care controls) — not a drop-in, but a comparator whose search predates a pooled trial cannot be scope-matched → `comparator_recency` field: `COMPARATOR_PREDATES_POOLED_TRIAL(names)`. Same for `omega3` (Yu 2022 searched to Sept 2020; OMEGA-REMODEL 2024 in the decomposition) and `glp1` (Giugliano 2021 vs SOUL 2025).
- **Selection failures**: `sglt2-primary-prevention-hf` Zhang 2021 "✓ same question" but includes DAPA-HF, EMPEROR-Reduced (HFrEF entry), CREDENCE (CKD entry) — all excluded by this protocol — and "comparator k stated in text = 3" is a parser misread of a sentence about an older meta (the paper includes 8 and tabulates them); `sglt2-ckd-progression` SMART-C "population match: True" while 7 of its 10 trials are CV/HF populations the protocol excludes, its endpoint is pure-kidney (no CV death) at 0.62, and it states "A systematic review was not performed" → `broader IPD consortium evidence supporting the same treatment effect`; `metformin-pcos` benchmarks the add-on pool against Cochrane's monotherapy contrast OR 2.64 [1.85, 3.75] instead of metformin+CC vs CC OR 1.65 [1.35, 2.03] (~22 studies) — and repeats it for GI harms (4.00 vs the add-on 4.26).
