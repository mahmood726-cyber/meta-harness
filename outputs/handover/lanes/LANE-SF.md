## Findings (search/screening audit #1, `ticagrelor-vs-clopidogrel-acs`; CLAIMED until re-measured on `ad5e7c66` objects)
1. **De facto criterion nobody wrote down.** PLATO and PHILO (pooled) are double-blind; TICAKOREA (800 ACS, open-label) and TREAT (3,799 STEMI post-fibrinolysis, 12-month clinical outcomes, open-label) are head-to-head ticagrelor-vs-clopidogrel RCTs absent from the universe. Today the effective blinding rule exists only because the seeded search returned blinded trials — a criterion that appears in neither the prose nor the config. **Sweep: for every page, compare the design characteristics (masking, comparator type, population subtype, timepoint) of the INCLUDED set against the protocol's stated design rule; flag `IMPLICIT_CRITERION(dimension, included_values ⊂ rule_values)` wherever the included set is narrower than the rule requires.** Same for population: TREAT (STEMI after fibrinolysis) is ACS; excluding it needs an explicit "primary-PCI or non-fibrinolysis ACS only" rule. Pipeline: `publication → trial family (NCT / trial name / registry link / shared arms) → ONE screening decision per family`; reports attach to the family with `publication_role`. Screening each PMID as an independent trial is the defect.
3. **Re-derive every count**: `publications screened → trial families → eligible trial families`, N named at each step, for all 32 pages; the page sentence is generated from the three numbers. Report the before (per-PMID) and after (per-family) counts side by side; expect large drops on pages with prolific trials (PLATO, FOURIER, EMPA-REG, DAPA-HF).
4. DISPERSE-2 must remain ELIGIBLE at screening — awkward extraction (shared-control multi-arm) is downstream and must never become effective absence at the screening layer; plant it.
