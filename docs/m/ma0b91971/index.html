Overview

Empagliflozin vs placebo for cardiovascular death or heart-failure hospitalisation in HFpEF/HFmrEF

In adults with heart failure and preserved or mildly reduced ejection fraction, does empagliflozin reduce the composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure versus placebo? (Double-blind placebo-controlled RCTs.)
