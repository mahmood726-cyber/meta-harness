Ticagrelor vs clopidogrel for major adverse cardiovascular events in acute coronary syndrome

In adults with acute coronary syndrome, does ticagrelor reduce major adverse cardiovascular events, defined as the composite of cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, or stroke, compared with clopidogrel?
