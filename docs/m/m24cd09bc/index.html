Abstract

Question. In adults with type 2 diabetes or at cardiovascular risk, do SGLT2 inhibitors reduce hospitalization for heart failure versus placebo? (Double-blind placebo-controlled cardiovascular outcome trials.)

A prospectively registered, fully reproducible review: the protocol was committed before synthesis (registration SHA UNCOMMITTED-); a registry-first search was screened by two independent rule screeners with adjudication (36 records assessed); every pooled number was extracted down a source ladder and verified against its committed source.
