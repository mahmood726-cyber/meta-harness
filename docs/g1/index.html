Decisions applied

  • D1-SWEEP-AACT-PRIMARY (2026-10-02, Mahmood (approval relayed 2026-10-05)): A trial's own posted results in a versioned AACT snapshot count as a PRIMARY source on the page (route SWEEP_AACT_PRIMARY), only with the recorded registry binding: NCT, the registry outcome title, the snapshot id + digest, the posted-N = randomised-N guard, and two-arm counts or a two-sided effect with its CI. Anything missing is not counted (fail-closed).
  • D2-ONE-TRIAL-SHARE (2026-10-05, captain under Mahmood's delegation (dispatch 2026-10-05)): ONE_COMPARABLE_TRIAL passes RESULT_AGREES only when the directly comparable pairs that AGREE carry at least half of the shared trials' participants AND every other shared pair is a named MEASURE_DIFFERENCE with the same conclusion. A pair without a participant count, or a shared pair that is neither, fails closed. One small trial never carries a topic. Participants come from the pair's arm counts, else from the trial's OWN held abstract stating its randomised N (verb before the number, never negated or future, exactly one N; span recorded) -- g1_tracker.stated_randomised_n (corpus: 35 of 296 comparator trials with a held abstract state one).
  • D3-COMPARATOR-POOLS-NO-RCT (2026-10-05, captain under Mahmood's delegation (dispatch 2026-10-05)): A comparator that pools no RCTs cannot be matched trial-for-trial by an RCT review: the topic stays in the denominator with status COMPARATOR_POOLS_NO_RCT, a span quoted verbatim from the comparator's own held record, and is listed on the page as not attainable against this comparator. Replacing the comparator is Mahmood's decision.
  • D4-NO-POOL-META-SECOND-SOURCE-ONLY (2026-10-05, g1/forest-reader lane rule (POOL_UNCHECKABLE)): A meta whose figure prints no pooled result cannot reproduce its own pool (no positive control): its rows count only as a SECOND source beside a gated meta or a primary, never alone (SECOND_SOURCE_ONLY).
