# VERIFY - metformin-pcos-ovulation

Primary outcome: ovulation rate at end of treatment.

Poolable trial quotes:

- Moll et al., PMID 16769748:
  > "The ovulation rate in the metformin group was 64% compared with 72% in the placebo group, a non-significant difference (risk difference - 8%, 95% confidence interval - 20% to 4%)."

- Ng et al., PMID 11473953:
  > "The median ovulation rate in the placebo group was 0% (range: 0--50%) after placebo only and 6.9% (range: 0--50%) after placebo and CC, whereas the corresponding rates in the metformin group were 0% (range: 0--22%) and 0% (range: 0--22%) respectively."

- Sturrock et al., PMID 11994052:
  > "There was no difference in the efficacy of clomiphene between the two groups with ovulation being induced in five (out of 12) metformin treated women and four (out of 14) placebo treated women, P=0.63."

- Kocak et al., PMID 12070543:
  > "A statistically significant increase in the rates of ovulation (68.6% versus 25%, p<0.05) and pregnancy (56.3% versus 16.6%, p<0.05) were observed in the metformin-clomiphene citrate group as compared with the placebo-clomiphene citrate controls."

- Vandermolen et al., PMID 11172832:
  > "In the metformin and placebo groups, 9 of 12 participants (75%) and 4 of 15 participants (27%) ovulated, and 6 of 11 participants (55%) and 1 of 14 participants (7%) conceived, respectively."

Notes:

- Quote sources: PubMed abstracts for listed PMIDs.
- Co-interventions differ across trials; no computation performed here.
